# Level 4 Labs — Ownership & Responsibility

The Level 4 track establishes the evidence and controls required to own an enterprise AI product.
It connects governance policy to runtime enforcement and named accountability.

## Planned lab sequence

| Lab | Capability | Verifiable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI security | Define threat and trust boundaries | Tested authentication, authorization, data, and tool controls |
| Prompt injection | Attack and defend context/tool boundaries | Layered mitigations and residual risk are documented |
| Human approval | Gate consequential actions | Approval is informed, attributable, authorized, and auditable |
| Audit logging | Reconstruct a material decision | Versions, context sources, tools, approvals, and outcome are traceable |
| Production readiness | Review the complete capability | Quality, reliability, security, operations, and ownership gate passes |

## Integrated mini-project

Produce a governed enterprise AI product proposal using the Level 3 system. Classify risk, assign
owners, implement approval and emergency-stop behavior, exercise an incident, and measure the target
business outcome.

## Completion gate

- Business, product, technical, security, and operational owners are named by role.
- Risk classification maps to enforceable preventive, detective, and corrective controls.
- Consequential action requires deterministic authorization and appropriate human approval.
- Audit, incident, kill-switch, change, and retirement procedures are tested or reviewed.
- Limitations, affected users, business value, ongoing cost, and review cadence are explicit.
